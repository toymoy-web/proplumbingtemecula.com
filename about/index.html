About Temecula Pro Plumbing

Temecula Pro Plumbing provides licensed plumbing repair, installation, and emergency service throughout Temecula, California and the surrounding Temecula Valley, including the areas covered on our service area page.

How We Work

Every call starts with an actual diagnosis, not a default recommendation. Whether that's electronic leak tracing for a slab leak, a camera run through a sewer line, or checking a water heater's specific failed component, the cause gets confirmed before a repair is quoted. A flat price is set once that diagnosis is complete and approved before any repair work begins, so there's no surprise line item added once a wall or a section of concrete is already open.

What We Handle

Our full service list covers drain cleaning and hydro jetting, water heater repair and tankless installation, leak detection and slab leak repair, sewer line repair, burst pipe and emergency response, toilet and faucet repair, garbage disposal repair, water softener and filtration service, and pool equipment leak detection. Same-day appointments and after-hours emergency dispatch are available seven days a week.

Licensing and Compliance

Temecula Pro Plumbing operates as a licensed California plumbing contractor. We do not use HTML contact forms. Every call routes directly to a live line rather than a web submission queue, which is part of why response times stay fast for genuine emergencies.

Questions Before You Call

Our blog covers practical, locally grounded plumbing information, from checking your own water meter for a hidden leak to understanding what Temecula's climate and water hardness actually mean for a home's plumbing. If a question isn't answered there, the fastest way to get a real answer is still a phone call.
