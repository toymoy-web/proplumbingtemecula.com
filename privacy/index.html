Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how Temecula Pro Plumbing ("we," "us," or "our") handles information collected through this website and through phone calls made to our business line.

Information We Collect

When you call our business line, we may collect your name, phone number, address, and a description of the service you're requesting, in order to schedule and provide that service. Our website does not use contact forms. We do not collect information through web-based form submissions. Standard web analytics tools may collect general, non-identifying information about site visits, such as which pages are viewed and general geographic region, for the purpose of understanding site usage.

How We Use Information

Information collected by phone is used to schedule, dispatch, and invoice plumbing services, and to follow up regarding work performed. We do not sell customer information to third parties.

Call Tracking

Phone numbers displayed on this site may be call-tracking numbers used to measure which marketing channels generate calls. Calls may be recorded or monitored for quality and training purposes. If a call is recorded, that will be disclosed at the start of the call where required by law.

Your Rights Under California Law

California residents have certain rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and related California privacy law regarding personal information we may hold, including the right to request disclosure of the categories of information collected and the right to request deletion, subject to legal and contractual exceptions. To make a request, contact us using the information on our contact page.

Third-Party Links

This site may link to third-party sites, including mapping services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party sites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to us via the phone number on our contact page.
