Pools and Irrigation Add Plumbing to Maintain

A backyard pool brings its own plumbing: supply and return lines, a pump and filter set, a heater, valves and the connections between them. Those lines run underground and under decking, so a leak there often shows as a pool that keeps losing water or a wet patch near the equipment pad rather than a visible burst. The pool equipment leak detection service exists for exactly that situation.

The same lots usually carry irrigation systems. A broken sprinkler line or failing valve can waste water and soften soil near foundations without obvious signs. A water bill that climbs with no change in habits is a useful early warning for either system.

A simple check anyone can do is the bucket test for the pool and a look at the meter with all water in the house turned off, to see whether it still moves. If it does, there is water going somewhere it should not.

FAQ

Do you work on pool plumbing in Crowne Hill?

Pool equipment leak detection is part of the service list, including pressure testing the lines between the equipment and the pool. If you think your pool is losing water, a bucket test can help tell evaporation from a leak before you call.

Is emergency service available in Crowne Hill?

Yes, Crowne Hill is covered any hour of any day. A burst line or a failed pool or irrigation fitting should be isolated at the valve first, then call (951) 467-1450.

How do I tell if my water bill is rising because of a leak?

Turn off every fixture and appliance, then check your water meter. If the dial or low-flow indicator is still moving, water is going somewhere. That is a good reason to schedule leak detection.
